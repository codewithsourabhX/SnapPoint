# SnapPoint – Survey & Rewards Platform

**Earn rewards by completing surveys, tasks, and games.**

[Website](https://snappoint.in)

## About

SnapPoint is an online survey and rewards platform that allows users to earn rewards by participating in surveys, completing tasks, and playing games.

The platform connects users with third-party market research opportunities through survey providers such as CPX Research. Users can explore available opportunities and earn rewards for successfully completing eligible activities.

The goal is to create a simple and accessible platform where users can turn their time and participation into rewards.

## Features

- **Online Surveys:** Access market research surveys through integrated survey providers.
- **CPX Research Integration:** Connect users with third-party survey opportunities.
- **Tasks & Offers:** Earn rewards by completing available activities.
- **Reward System:** Track earnings generated through eligible activities.
- **User Accounts:** Register and access the platform through a personal account.
- **Responsive Interface:** Access the platform across desktop and mobile devices.

## How It Works

1. Create an account on SnapPoint.
2. Explore available surveys, tasks, and games.
3. Select an activity and complete its requirements.
4. Eligible completions are validated through the relevant provider.
5. Rewards are credited according to the platform's reward rules.

## Security & Reliability

The platform's security considerations include:

- Email verification
- CAPTCHA-based bot protection
- Server-side reward validation
- Webhook verification
- Unique transaction IDs to prevent duplicate reward credits
- Suspicious activity monitoring

Sensitive API credentials and webhook secrets should be stored in server-side environment variables and never exposed in client-side code.
